FAQ

Quick answers for busy teams.

Is Matelligence a database or an evaluation tool?

Both. It helps you discover candidates and then evaluate them with normalized specs, assumptions, and test planning—so decisions are consistent and traceable.

Where does the data come from?

Matelligence is designed to ingest manufacturer datasheets, supplier catalogs, and your internal test results. The platform tracks source links and confidence notes per property.

Can we use this for sustainability targets?

Yes. Compare candidates by recycled content, CO₂ footprint, supplier region, and compliance constraints, then document trade-offs for stakeholders.

How do we get started?

Tell us the product, requirements, and constraints. We’ll set up a workspace and run a first materials shortlisting session with your team.
